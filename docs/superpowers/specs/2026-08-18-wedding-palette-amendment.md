# Amendment — Wedding visual palette and restraint

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: Approved concept amendment

## Goal

Align the entire celebration site with the real wedding palette while keeping the product elegant, editorial, and restrained.

## Core palette direction

Use four families across the site:

- warm beige / ivory — primary light surface and text-on-dark tone
- deep muted green — structural brand color and calm anchor
- cinnamon / warm brown — secondary accent and material warmth
- restrained dark wine / brick red — emphasis, alerts, reveal moments, and selected highlights

Avoid bright saturated red, emerald green, orange-brown, pure wedding-white everywhere, and excessive black.

## Tone

The site should feel like a premium event identity, not a rustic autumn wedding template.

Visual references should lean toward:

- editorial print design
- premium invitation stationery
- refined hospitality/menu design
- restrained railway ephemera for Viktor Train

Avoid:

- kraft-paper clichés
- leaves, branches, flowers as generic decoration
- faux vintage textures everywhere
- watercolor wedding graphics
- excessive gradients
- gold-on-black luxury clichés
- cartoon visuals

## Surface hierarchy

Recommended hierarchy:

- guest hub and tickets: warm beige/ivory dominant with green/cinnamon structure
- voting pages: beige or very dark green surfaces with restrained Liza/Viktor differentiation
- admin: more utilitarian dark green/charcoal with beige text for legibility
- projector: adapt per module; do not force one background across everything
- video premiere: near-black cinematic state remains intentional exception
- Mortal Kombat: darker, higher-contrast interpretation of the same palette, with wine/red accents, rather than unrelated neon arcade colors
- Viktor Train: beige ticket stock feel, deep green typography/lines, cinnamon and wine accents

## Color usage rules

- Green is the primary identity color.
- Beige/ivory is the breathing-space color and should keep the interface light enough overall.
- Cinnamon is for secondary accents, dividers, metadata, cards, and warm highlights.
- Wine/brick red is scarce and meaningful: active states, important calls, champion/reveal moments, or countdown emphasis.
- Do not assign a different arbitrary color to every carriage/team. Carriages are distinguished mainly by number/name, typography, icon/mark, and layout—not rainbow coding.

## Carriage/team visual identity

Because carriage and team are the same entity, do not color-code the five teams with five unrelated colors.

All carriages belong to one coherent wedding identity. Distinguish them using:

- large carriage number
- subtle numeral pattern or monogram
- unique short label if added later
- optional small geometric marker

Keep the same wedding palette across all five.

## Accessibility

All text/background combinations must meet practical contrast for phone and projector use. Beige-on-green and ivory-on-wine may be used only where contrast is sufficient. Small cinnamon text on beige should be avoided when contrast is weak.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Overall site visibly matches the wedding's green-beige-cinnamon-red palette.
2. The result remains restrained and premium rather than themed/decorative.
3. Mortal Kombat and Premiere may become darker without breaking the shared identity.
4. Carriages/teams are not represented as rainbow-colored groups.
5. The same visual system works on guest phone, owner admin phone, and projector.
